A Cooperative English Adventure

Teacher controls the explorer. Player 1, Player 2 and the team unlock the world by speaking English.

How to play

🎮 Teacher: move with A/D or ←/→ and jump with W, ↑ or Space.
🔵 Player 1: answers blue challenges.
🟣 Player 2: answers purple challenges.
🤝 Team: discuss together before the teacher clicks.
🎤 Speaking: students answer aloud; the teacher accepts or asks them to try again.
🏆 Goal: collect gems, unlock all three worlds and defeat the Grammar Guardian.
